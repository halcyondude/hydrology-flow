Smith Pond is a distinct pond next to Cook's Pond in Worcester, MA. The dam (NID MA00123, earth, 15 ft, high hazard) sits between the two ponds.
A now-consolidated group, of which Matt is a member, manages both ponds and the dam. Cook's Pond borders Donker Farm (48/50 Tory Fort Ln).
Matt formerly lived at 65 Tory Fort Lane, a useful ground-truth location.
Smith Pond is NOT MassDEP MA51156 (Leicester), and NOT the Northborough NID "Smith Pond Dam".

**Why:** Matt clarified this on 2026-10-17. The handoff had two wrong hypotheses.
**How to apply:** Model both ponds as linked reservoirs on either side of MA00123. Confirm from NHD which pond is upstream of the dam.
